# Multiple spell pools Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development or superpowers:executing-plans. Already approved for continuous execution.

**Goal:** Represent spell acquisition, preparation and distinct slot/resource pools accurately.
**Architecture:** Extend schema-2 casting policies with source-owned tier tables and editable loadouts. Keep spell access independent from available slot capacity; project rather than recalculate in UI/PDF.
**Tech Stack:** Swift, SwiftUI, PDFKit, Node, standalone checks.
**Spec:** `docs/designs/2026-11-12-rules-expansion.md`

## Global Constraints
- Keep existing schema-1 packs and characters readable and evaluable with their current semantics.
- Ordinary spellcasting and pact/custom pools remain distinct.
- Higher available slots do not automatically grant knowledge of higher-level spells.
- Spending slots/rest simulation is out of scope.
- Public examples remain original; private definitions remain imported and private.

## Review Focus
1. Two casting abilities cannot be collapsed into one misleading DC.
2. Changing preparation must not erase learned spells or spellbook membership.
3. Missing recovery/source data cannot silently merge two counters.
4. Always-granted spells cannot consume ordinary learned/prepared allowance incorrectly.
5. PDF overflow must preserve source, policy and every tier.
