# Equipment and loadouts Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development or superpowers:executing-plans. Already approved for continuous execution.

**Goal:** Calculate inventory/loadout effects and weapon summaries without introducing combat resolution.
**Architecture:** Declarative item catalog plus saved quantities/equipment modes feed a pure loadout evaluator after historical character replay. One selected AC formula plus compatible shield/modifiers, never stacked alternative defenses.
**Tech Stack:** Swift, SwiftUI, Codable, PDFKit, standalone checks.
**Spec:** `docs/designs/2026-11-12-rules-expansion.md`

## Global Constraints
- Show attacks/formulas, not automatic dice resolution or target damage.
- Require explicit choices for alternatives such as wielding mode; do not equip every owned item or stack incompatible defenses.
- Public examples remain original; private definitions remain imported and private.
- Keep existing schema-1 packs and characters readable and evaluable with their current semantics.
- Unmodeled magic-item effects remain explicitly descriptive/manual, not silently automated.

## Review Focus
1. Equipped and carried are distinct; zero quantity must never grant benefits.
2. Shield, two-handed use and attunement cannot form impossible combinations silently.
3. Repeated evaluation/equip/unequip must apply each modifier exactly once.
4. Earlier-level projection evaluates current equipment against earlier training.
5. Starting money and equipment cannot both be granted from one exclusive choice.

### Task 1: Deliver inventory and native equipment editing

**Files:** Create `Sources/FolioCore/Equipment.swift`, `Sources/FolioApp/EquipmentEditor.swift`, `Tests/FolioCoreTests/EquipmentChecks.swift`; extend contracts/validation/catalog/replay, character UI, `PrintableSheet.swift`, PDF checks, schema/examples/private adapter/coverage/docs.

**Interfaces:** Item definitions own kind, properties, bounded numeric stats, training category, armor formula, hand modes, quantity weight/cost, attunement policy, support status and typed modifiers. Build inventory owns source-qualified item ID, quantity, carried/equipped/attuned and selected wielding mode; currency stores denominations. `EquipmentEngine.evaluate` consumes replayed traits/scores and returns AC/attack/damage/speed/stealth/load/attunement summaries and issues, not mutations.

**Primary evidence checked:** [SRD 5.2.1](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf), weapon properties p.89, armor p.92, attunement p.102, glossary pp.177–178. Revised untrained shield use does not grant its AC bonus; untrained armor restricts casting and certain checks, not simply AC. Heavy weapon penalties depend on Strength/Dexterity thresholds in 2024, unlike the legacy size rule (verify legacy details when implementing). Default attunement is three, with no duplicate copy of the same item; explicit class exceptions may modify eligibility/count. Carrying capacity depends on size and Strength; do not treat worn equipment as weightless. Keep these as edition/profile and typed data behavior, not display-name checks. Add regression cases for these differences and canonical-item duplicates.

**Legacy differences verified:** The supplied legacy SRD equipment rules specify Small-creature disadvantage for Heavy weapons, not Strength/Dexterity thresholds. A legacy shield still grants its AC bonus without proficiency, but incurs the armor/shield proficiency penalties; [official 2014 Basic Rules, Choose Equipment](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/step-by-step-characters#5ChooseEquipment) explicitly applies those penalties to armor and shields. Heavy armor's Strength-based speed penalty is a separate rule, not the Heavy weapon property. Test these distinctions against revised behavior.
